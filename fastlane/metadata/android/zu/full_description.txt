Umhlaba ulindile! Ingabe uzokwakha impucuko yakho ibe umbuso oyomelana novivinyo lwesikhathi?

Imvume "yokufinyelela ngokugcwele kunethiwekhi" iyadingeka ekulandeni okuqalwa ngumsebenzisi nasesicini sabadlali abaningi. Zonke ezinye izimvume ezisohlwini zifakwa ngokuzenzakalela yi-API esetshenziselwa izaziso zamathuba omdlalo wabadlali abaningi. Izimvume zenethiwekhi zisetshenziselwa ukufaka ohlwini ama-mod, ukulanda ama-mod, ukulanda umculo kanye nokulayisha noma ukulanda imidlalo yabadlali abaningi. Unciv ayiqalisi okunye ukuxhumana nge-inthanethi.
